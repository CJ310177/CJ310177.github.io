01About

I am Junchao Cui, a researcher in computer vision working on worldwide image and video geo-localization — inferring where on Earth visual content was captured, from street level up to the continent.

My work covers dual-view contrastive representation learning, metric geographic structure modeling, and hierarchical graph reasoning over geographic scales, often augmented by large multimodal models (LMMs). It has been published at ICME 2026 and Information Processing and Management (IPM), and I authored the first survey on worldwide image geo-localization. Feel free to reach out for collaboration or discussion.

i Worldwide image geo-localization single-image location estimation
ii Global video geo-localization temporal & hierarchical cues
iii Multimodal large models retrieval-augmented LMM reasoning
