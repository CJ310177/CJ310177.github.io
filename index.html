About

I am a researcher in computer vision working on worldwide image and video geo-localization — the task of predicting the geographic location of visual content captured anywhere on the planet, from street level up to the continent.

My recent work explores dual-view contrastive learning for image geo-localization (DualGeo, ICME 2026), metric geographic structure injection (GeoMetric), and hierarchical graph attention for global video geo-localization (GeoGAT). I also authored the first survey on worldwide image geo-localization, accepted by Information Processing and Management (IPM).

Open to collaboration on geo-localization, multimodal learning, and retrieval-augmented inference.

Junchao Cui

The first comprehensive survey of worldwide image geo-localization, covering the task formulation, datasets, feature-based and learning-based methods, hierarchical prediction strategies, and the emerging role of large multimodal models — together with open challenges and future directions for the field.

Preprint · arXiv:2609.39128 · September 2026

GeoGAT: Bidirectional Temporal Sampling Meets Hierarchical Graph Attention for Global Video Geo-localization

Junchao Cui, Xuanzi Ma, Wenqi Shi, Hangyu Li, Biru Zhu, Chong Fu, Xiangyang Luo

Global video geo-localization aims to infer the geographic location of a video worldwide, evaluated across four geographic hierarchies. Existing methods typically employ one-way uniform sampling and train independent classifiers per hierarchy, losing key geographic cues and causing prediction conflicts. GeoGAT integrates bidirectional temporal sampling with graph attention networks: forward and offset-reversed frame sequences build complementary spatiotemporal features, which are passed through a predefined geographic hierarchy graph with structure-aware message passing and a dual-constraint pruning mechanism. We also introduce GeoGAT10k (9,720 multi-shot edited videos from 166 cities). GeoGAT eliminates hierarchical conflicts entirely and improves over the strongest baseline by 2.6 points (city level) on CityGuessr68k and over 24 points on GeoGAT10k.

GeoMetric: Injecting Metric Geographic Structure into Worldwide Image Geo-Localization

Junchao Cui, Xuanzi Ma, Wenqi Shi, Nan Wu, Biru Zhu, Xiangyang Luo

In existing worldwide image geo-localization, GPS coordinates serve only as training supervision and the distance relationships among locations never enter the learned representation, so appearance-based methods mistake distant look-alikes for the true location. GeoMetric is a retrieval-based framework that encodes GPS coordinates relationally: a Transformer-based GPS encoder with distance-aware location attention, a trimodal contrastive objective aligning images, geo-textual descriptions, and GPS embeddings, and a retrieval-augmented inference stage that supplies large multimodal models with contrastive candidate context. It consistently outperforms state-of-the-art methods on IM2GPS, IM2GPS3k, YFCC4k, and YFCC26k.

ICME 2026 · IEEE International Conference on Multimedia and Expo · arXiv:2604.25533

DualGeo: A Dual-View Framework for Worldwide Image Geo-localization

Junchao Cui, Wenqi Shi, Shaoyong Du, Hang He, Xuanzi Ma, Hao Tang, Xiangyang Luo

Existing worldwide image geo-localization methods rely on visual features sensitive to environmental variations and lack effective post-processing to filter outlier candidates. DualGeo is a two-stage framework: it first builds a geo-representational foundation by fusing image and semantic segmentation features via bidirectional cross-attention, aligned with GPS coordinates through dual-view contrastive learning; it then performs geo-cognitive refinement by re-ranking retrieved candidates with geographic clustering and feeding them into large multimodal models for final coordinate prediction. DualGeo outperforms state-of-the-art methods on IM2GPS, IM2GPS3k, and YFCC4k.
